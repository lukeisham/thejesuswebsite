# Classifier Decisions Log

The dated record of decisions on the Wikipedia classifier's labelling rules, its labeller and
its activation gate. It says what was decided, when, by whom and why, so that a future agent
does not undo a ruling it takes for a mistake. The current rules are in
`DATA_INTERPRETATION_TIERS.md`, and the gate is in `CLASSIFIER_DIAGNOSIS.md`.

This log is the classifier half of `setup/Decisions_log.md` (D-056). The test for what goes in,
how to write an entry, the Status wording and the shared `D-NNN` series are all in that file. A
classifier decision that also changes the site, the deploy or a permission goes there instead.
